Architectural Pillars

Engineered for determinism, portability, and zero corporate hallucination
LAYERED SUBSYSTEMS
Pillar 01 · Decision Intelligence

RLCD Calibrated Engine

Balances structural prompt priors ($P_{\text{struct}}$) against learned policy weights ($P_{\text{policy}}$) using Jensen-Shannon Divergence ($JSD$) and normalized Shannon entropy ($H_{\text{norm}}$). Employs an intent guard that strictly blocks unprompted Executive Summary drift.

  • Dual-perspective consensus scoring
  • Calibrated risk gates (Confident vs Ambiguous)
  • Temperature scaling & Brier scoring
  • REINFORCE policy gradient adaptation
Pillar 02 · Retrieval & Grounding

Local-First Hybrid Corpus

Stores chunked source documents with SHA-256 deduplication in SQLite. Combines lexical BM25 matching via FTS5 with dense MiniLM vector similarity using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF). Queries are executed per-section to prevent generic text dilution.

  • Zero-network /create by default
  • Section-level query isolation
  • Deduplicated URL provenance tracking
  • Honest grounding coverage reporting
Pillar 03 · Authoring & Packaging

Dual OpenXML Engines

High-fidelity word processing via WordCraft MCP (live desktop GUI composition, PDF rendering, and AST inspection) paired with a 100% pure standard library / Rust fallback engine ensuring zero C-dependency execution across NixOS and Linux.

  • Complete ECMA-376 DrawingML packaging
  • Native ZIP media and relationship tracking
  • Anti-Slop typography (no Inter, tight tracking)
  • Publication-grade PDF export support

Design Archetypes & Editorial Discipline

        
Calibrated document profiles governed by the Anti-Slop editorial standard

      

      

        EDITORIAL PROFILES
      

    


    

      

        Technical Report
        
Analytical, Structural & Rigorous

        

          Engineered for systems specifications, architectural whitepapers, and operational evaluations. Uses Navy/Slate palettes, Times New Roman or Georgia typography, explicit hardware decomposition matrices, and procedural flow steps.
        

        

          Key Elements: System topology, verification matrices, code fragments, performance benchmarks.
        

      


      

        Academic Monograph
        
Scholarly, Formal & Discursive

        

          Multi-chapter analytical treatises. Features abstract summaries, theoretical foundations, related literature surveys, formal mathematical formulations, empirical discussion, and comprehensive literature citations.
        

        

          Key Elements: Chapter page breaks, numbered equations, methodology flowcharts, literature cited.
        

      


      

        Executive Summary
        
Strategic, Decision-Oriented & Concise

        

          Targeted at senior leadership and board members. Focuses strictly on strategic context, key operational observations, risk exposure vectors, and actionable forward recommendations without technical minutiae.
        

        

          Key Elements: Executive KPI dashboards, risk severity matrices, 0.8-inch margins, signature callouts.
        

      


      

        Informative / Minimalist Editorial
        
Engaging, Authoritative & Elegant

        

          Understated editorial style for natural science treatises and general readership compendiums. Eliminates artificial titles, utilizing generous line spacing, clean subheadings, and authentic photographic figures.
        

        

          Key Elements: Clean single-term titles, authentic figure captions, biological taxonomy tables.
